42 Foundry Lane, Old Mill District

We started with six stools and a charcoal grill.

In 2016, Marcus Hale turned a corner butcher's counter into a steakhouse. He had spent ten years cooking in other people's kitchens and wanted one where the beef did the talking.

We still buy whole cuts of beef from three family farms within 150 miles, age them for 45 days in our salt-brick cellar, and cook everything over oak and charcoal. The only seasoning is sea salt.

By morning the same room becomes a café. The oven that bakes our dinner rolls also bakes the croissants, and you never need a reservation.

Nothing fancy happens to the meat. It gets time, heat and salt.

Marcus Hale, chef and owner

Our cuts

Every steak is dry-aged in our cellar for 45 days, seasoned with sea salt and cooked over oak and charcoal. Tap a cut to see it.

Every cut comes with bone-marrow butter and one side. We cook to your taste, and we suggest medium-rare.

By day, it's a café.

Come in for a flat white and a croissant, or stay for eggs on sourdough. Everything is baked and made here, and you never need to book.

Walk-ins welcome. Last orders are 30 minutes before we close.
